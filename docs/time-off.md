# Time Off Requests

!!! tldr "TL;DR"
    All time off goes in **Qgenda before the quarterly deadline**. Generally, only one fellow off at a time. Major meetings and holidays have specific exceptions below.

## How to request

All requests for time off — vacation **and** meetings — must be entered into **Qgenda prior to the quarterly deadline**. The section admin will send a reminder when the deadline is approaching.

!!! pro-tip "Plan ahead"
    Spreading vacation across the year is a much better strategy than trying to fit everything in toward the end. Late requests routinely don't get approved.

---

## How many fellows can be off at once?

**Default rule: only one fellow off the schedule at a time.** Exceptions below.

??? abstract "Second fellow off — case by case"
    Up to two fellows can be off at a time, but **not for recurring consecutive days or weeks**.

    !!! warning "Approval required"
        If you see in Qgenda that you'd be the **second fellow** requesting a day off, you must email **Dr. Ross and Natalie Roisum** and **wait for approval before committing** (e.g. before buying airline tickets).

??? abstract "RSNA week"
    Split coverage:

    - **Mon–Tue:** 2–3 fellows
    - **Wed–Fri:** 2–3 fellows

    Depending on staffing.

??? abstract "Other major meetings (SSR, ARRS, etc.)"
    Up to **two** fellows may attend if they've submitted a scientific abstract for presentation.

    **SSR exception:** up to **three** fellows may attend if presenting a podium presentation.

??? abstract "Holidays — Christmas / New Year"
    Split coverage:

    - **2–3 fellows off Christmas week**, the others off **New Year's week** (use vacation days)
    - **Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Day** are holidays — only the call team works
    - **New Year's Eve is NOT a holiday** — 2–3 fellows must be on service. One MSK attending on, but it's a regular workday for clinical fellows.

??? abstract "Terminal vacation (last 5 weekdays of June)"
    Up to **4 fellows** may take terminal vacation, but **one must remain to cover WIMR contrast**.

## Blackout periods

You **cannot** take vacation during:

- **ISS** week
- **RSNA** week
- **SSR** week
- **ARRS** week
- **Spring Break** weeks for Madison and Middleton schools

See [conference dates](conferences.md) for specific weeks this year.

## Bereavement, interviewing, sick days

| Situation | Policy |
|---|---|
| Death of immediate family (grandparents, parents, uncle, aunt, spouse) | Up to **3 paid clinic days** of bereavement leave |
| Job interviews | Use **vacation days** — not sick or academic |
| Sick day | Notify per [communication policy](communication.md). If we pull a fellow from academic to cover, you owe them academic time back. |

!!! warning "Don't stake claims"
    Don't request more days off than you have vacation days as a way to "stake your claim" on certain days. Out of courtesy to your co-fellows.

## Meeting attendance — how the days are budgeted

You get up to:

- **5 days for Meeting Presenting** (meetings where you have an accepted project)
- **5 days for Meeting Attending** (no presentation obligation)

= **10 meeting days total**, regardless of how many projects you complete or how many opportunities to present.

!!! note
    Vacation days may be used for additional meetings, subject to the standard fellow-coverage rules above.

!!! warning "Virtual attendance"
    If you're considering attending virtually instead of in person, **get approval before committing to the meeting**.

## After the schedule is made — last-minute changes

If something comes up after the schedule is finalized, the workflow:

1. **Try to trade first.** Find another fellow to come off academic to cover your clinical service. Pay them back later by giving up one of your academic days.
2. **If you're already academic** and need to fly out, those days can convert to vacation.
3. **If no one can sub for you,** contact the attending(s) for the affected services. Explain the reason. Ask if it's okay to leave the service without a fellow.
    - For job interviews and family emergencies, the answer is almost always yes
    - Occasionally another accommodation is needed
4. **Once cleared,** email the MSK Administrative Assistant + affected faculty and fellows + Drs. Lee and Ross.

---

## Unused vacation

Since you're academic staff, unused vacation can be **paid out**. A full day = 8 hours; your monthly salary breaks down to an hourly rate and gets paid out per leftover hours.
