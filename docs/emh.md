## Ultrasound

The EMH rotation covers one room of ultrasound (two rooms on Wednesdays). Preview cases and run the list with the sonographer just as when at [SMC](smc.md).

---

## Spine protocoling

EMH owns protocoling for **all** MSK spine studies. Do them in batches to keep the list clear. See [Spine imaging workflow](spine-workflow.md) when unsure if a case belongs to Neuro.

---

## Tips

- Keep an eye out for ED/inpatient cross-sectional studies — this is your primary responsibility. Share cases when a Bone 2 resident is on, but make sure they get resulted promptly.
