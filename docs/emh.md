## Ultrasound

- The day before, check the EMH US schedule and flag any problems.
- In the morning, run the US list with the sonographer (~7–8 cases/day).
- You cover all MSK US at EMH (diagnostic and procedures). EMH US is on the 2nd floor near the reading room.

---

## Fluoro

PAs cover fluoro-guided procedures. **You're welcome to participate in any case** — small joints especially. Faculty know this and will cover other work in the meantime. There may also be an occasional US-guided biopsy for the fellow.

Read your own MR arthrograms (the ones you injected).

---

## ED / inpatient

The EMH team is **primary** for ED and inpatient cross-sectionals. In the morning, that includes studies prelim'd by the resident overnight. Document any changes per [discrepancies policy](discrepancies.md).

When MSK is on the emergency spine schedule, any spine exam read by the resident as normal needs faculty sign-off by **9:00 AM** — help facilitate this.

Share 1–2 overnight joint studies with the Bone 2 resident when they arrive at 8:30 AM. Save good call cases to teach them.

---

## Spine protocoling

EMH owns protocoling for **all** MSK spine studies. Do them in batches to keep the list clear. See [Spine imaging workflow](spine-workflow.md) when unsure if a case belongs to Neuro.

---

## Before leaving

Check with the MRI and CT techs to confirm contrast studies are done. Otherwise stay until 4:30 PM to cover contrast reactions.
