## Worklists

- **MSK OP MR & CT** — your main list. Read oldest first. No cherry-picking — same expectation for rotating residents and MR fellows.
- **MSK PROTOCOL** — joint MR and CT. Protocol in batches throughout the day. Use the indication on the request, but check the EMR if it's unclear.
- **MSK ALL** — watch for occasional ED/inpatient MSK US (~2/month). Read these early in case a non-MSK sonographer scanned and a repeat is needed.

EMH picks up ≥10 outpatient MR/CT and SMC ≥5 to help decompress your list.

---

## Spine ownership

**Outpatient spine MR/CT** is shared with Neuroradiology. We read Ortho spine, Rehab spine, family medicine, and outside providers. Neuro reads Neurology, Oncology, Neurosurgery, internal medicine. See [Spine imaging workflow](spine-workflow.md) when in doubt.

---

## Tips

- Learn to be efficient and move through cases quickly — goal of 30/day by mid-year
- Learn from everyone's cases, even the ones you're not dictating
- Teach and share cases with other learners — supporting the residents is fun and benefits you both
