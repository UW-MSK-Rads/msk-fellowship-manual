# Daily Workflow

!!! tldr "TL;DR"
    Six services, shared work list, everyone helps finish before **4:30 PM**. Schedule lives in Qgenda.

## Where the schedule lives

The full clinical schedule is at **[radiology.wisc.edu](https://www.radiology.wisc.edu)** → Intranet → Schedules → Clinical Schedule, or download the **Qgenda** app for your phone.

## The six services

You'll rotate through these:

| Service | Location | Typical week |
|---|---|---|
| [Procedures at UH](procedures-uh.md) | G3/346 | Most weeks have you on the same service |
| Float at UH | G3/346 | Tuesday AM + alternating Friday AM |
| Sports/Spine at UH | MFCB Turski Education Center | — |
| ED/Bone at UH | — | — |
| East Madison Hospital (EMH) | EMH | — |
| Science Drive Medical Center (SMC) | SMC | — |

Over the year you spend roughly equal time on each. Especially early in the year, you'll stay on the same service for most of a week to settle in.

## The shared list rule

Each service has its core responsibilities, but ultimately the MSK section uses a **shared work list**. The expectation: regardless of your assignment, everyone stays to help finish the list until all studies completed (marked as reviewed) before **4:30 PM** are read out.

!!! warning "Watch MSK All"
    Volume varies by service, but the most important thing is everyone pitching in toward end of day. **Keep an eye on the "MSK All" list throughout the day regardless of your service assignment** so you can help out as needed.
