# Wellness Resources

!!! tldr "TL;DR"
    Confidential support is available for clinical, professional, or personal challenges. Use any of the resources below — none of them require going through the program.

## Behavioral Health Stress Management

Direct access to confidential, professional support.

- Call **(608) 233-3575**, Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM. Identify yourself as a UW Health employee.

## Peer Support

One-to-one with a peer to discuss clinical, professional, or personal challenges.

- Email **peersupport@uwhealth.org**.

## SupportLinc

Confidential individual assessment and consultation for everyday personal issues.

- Create an account using code **UWHEALTHGME** or call **(888) 986-5464**.

## SMPH Employee Assistance Office

Personal counseling, including help interpreting insurance benefits.

- Call **(608) 263-2987** or email **eao@mailplus.wisc.edu**.
