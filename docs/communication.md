# Communication Expectations

| Channel | Cadence | What lives there |
|---|---|---|
| `uwhealth.org` email | **Twice/day** weekdays | Section communications |
| `wisc.edu` email | **Once/week** | HR, mandatory training |
| Pager | Always-on during clinical | Urgent clinical |

## When to give faculty a heads-up

!!! note "Day before"
    Notify your faculty the day before if you'll be **arriving late** to service (e.g. journal club or off-site morning conference).

!!! note "As far in advance as possible"
    Notify faculty if you'll be **absent** from clinical service. Follow the [time off request policy](time-off.md).

!!! danger "Calling in sick"
    Notify, in this order:

    1. Clinical team
    2. Fellowship Director
    3. Section Chief
    4. Administrative Assistant
