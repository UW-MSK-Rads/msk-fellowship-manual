# Science Drive Medical Center

!!! tldr "The Basics"
    - **Arrive:** by **7:30 AM** to run the list with the sonographers and attending
    - **Primary role:** scheduled US and fluoro cases
    - **Secondary role:** ≥5 outpatient MR/CT off Sports/Spine, dictate radiographs, help with spine MRI, read your own MR arthrograms

<div class="rotation-page" markdown>

---

## Hands-on scanning is the point

MSK US scans and US-guided injections run in two rooms at SMC, 5 days a week (US 1 starts 8:10, US 2 starts 7:40). **This is your main hands-on US opportunity all year — be proactive about it.**

Sonographers are willing to teach but need to keep the day moving. The schedule has scanning time built in for fellows; ask for it.

---

## Workflow

- Run the list with each sonographer before the day starts — they should come to the reading room.
- Urgent cases get added from Sports Medicine Clinic; check with the sonographer for add-on slots.
- You and the rotating resident cover fluoro injections (cap the resident at two). On Fridays, PA Ryan Schuett usually covers fluoro.
- Read MR arthrograms you injected.

---

## Sharing with trainees

- Radiology residents rotate through some months — split procedures, involve them in interesting diagnostic cases.
- Sports medicine fellows rotate 2–3 half-days/month and tend to engage with scanning.
- Ortho interns spend a few days in the US RR — bring them into discussions.

---

## Coverage

If you're at a mandatory conference on an SMC day, **email faculty before 4 PM the day before** so they can plan to cover. Some faculty don't check email after hours.

---

## Before leaving

Confirm with the MRI techs that contrast studies are done. Otherwise stay (usually done by 4:30, rarely later than 5:00) to cover contrast reactions.
