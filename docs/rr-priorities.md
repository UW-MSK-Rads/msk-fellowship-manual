# MSK Reading Room Priorities

!!! tldr "TL;DR"
    Each rotation has a primary, secondary, and final read assignment. When you're caught up on yours, work down the list and converge on **MSK All** (chronological).

## Priorities by reading room

| Reading Room | On-site / hands-on | Primary | Secondary | Final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sports/Spine** | UH US (rare) | Outpatient MR/CT | All DR | MSK All ǂ |
| **SMC** | SMC US, inj, MRa | SMC DR | >5 outpatient MR & CT | MSK All ǂ |
| **EMH** | EMH US | Inpatient/ED MR & CT, EMH DR | >10 outpatient MR & CT | MSK All ǂ |
| **Procedures** | MP1, MRa | UH & Clinic DR | SMC & EMH DR | MSK All ǂ |
| **ED/Bone** | MP2 injections | UH & EMH ED DR | MSK All DR | MSK All ǂ |

ǂ chronological

## Notes

- If no ED rad on service, UH & EMH ED DR is #1 for everyone.
- Outside studies, DHC, HERI, 1 S. Park, GHC → assigned read pool.
