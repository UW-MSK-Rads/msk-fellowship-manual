# Teaching Conferences

<div class="conferences-page" markdown>

!!! note "Conference logistics"
    Conferences appear on your `uwhealth.org` Outlook calendar with links to join via Teams. Notify your attending the day before if a conference will put you on service late.

## At a glance

| Conference | When | Prep / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Good Case Conference (GCC)** | Weekly · Thu 7:15 AM | Bring 1–2 cases. Arrive early to get logged in. |
| **Vertebroplasty** | Monthly · Tue or Thu 7:15 AM (replaces GCC) | Dr. Cooley sends a form to collect procedure cases. |
| **Rheumatology** | Bimonthly · usually Wed 7:30 AM | Assigned fellow gives a short didactic and reviews cases with the attending. Build off the existing presentations on the shared drive. |
| **Ortho tumor** | Bimonthly · Tue 6:30 AM | Critical for building comfort with tumor cases. |
| **Pathology** | Quarterly · usually Wed 7:30 AM | Assigned fellow shows radiology findings of a case. Review with the attending ahead of time. |
| **Journal club** | Quarterly · time varies | Review assigned articles ahead of time and be ready to discuss. |
| **Scope correlation** | 3–5x/year · Thu 7:15 AM (replaces GCC) | Each fellow does one per year. |
| **Sports medicine** *(optional)* | Weekly | Scan the ortho admin's weekly email for high-yield sessions — grand rounds, radiology-related presentations. |
| **Resident lectures** *(optional)* | Varies | Listen in when you can. |
