## The day before — case review checklist

Print the schedule from Health Link and make notes for each procedure. Discuss uncertain cases with faculty *now* — not the morning of.

For every case, review:

- [ ] **Procedure order** and any comments
- [ ] **Recent clinical note** from the ordering provider (sometimes the order doesn't reflect what they want — right vs. left, steroid vs. anesthetic only, correct level)
- [ ] **Previous imaging** (use this to plan your approach)
- [ ] **Previous procedure notes** if this is a repeat (look for tips on setup, needle gauge/length, patient anxiety)
- [ ] **Allergies, medications, comorbidities**
- [ ] **Transitional spinal anatomy and numbering**

## Sedation cases

For procedures requiring conscious sedation (vertebroplasties, bone biopsies, ablations), there are extra steps.

### Night before

- Place pre-procedure orders for admission to the **10-bed unit**
- The MSK Nurse (Erica Timm, **263-6871**, pager **9585**) can help if needed

### Morning of

- See the patient in the 10-bed pre/post area
- Obtain consent, perform a focused H&P
- Document the H&P in Health Link
- Document a **separate attestation note** indicating you evaluated them prior to sedation
- Someone can walk you through this the first time

### After the procedure

- Patients return to 10-bed for recovery
- Place post-procedure orders **immediately** after the procedure (order sets exist in HL)
- For biopsies, place surgical pathology orders prior to the procedure.
